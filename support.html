Frequently asked

Where is my progress saved?

On your iPhone only (it saves every 20 seconds and whenever you leave the game). Deleting the app deletes your progress; there is no account or cloud save.

How do I move and look around?

Your left thumb walks (a joystick appears where you touch); your right thumb drags to look around. Your tool picks up trash on its own whenever some is in reach. Settings → View switches between first and third person.

My bin is full. What now?

Follow the green arrow to the nearest recycling point and walk into the green ring. Far from one? The Empty here button sends a pickup truck for a short video.

Why do I see ads?

Only when you choose to. Shop → Free (or the green ▶ FREE button) lists bonuses you can get for watching a short video: Double Speed, Double Coins, a Bag of Coins, a full TURBO or a pickup truck. The game never shows an ad on its own. You can change how Google may use your data any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices.

There is no sound.

Check the volume, the silent switch and the in-game Settings (music and sound).

The game feels slow on my phone.

The game lowers its detail by itself when the phone struggles. Closing other apps helps too.

Can I start over?

Settings → Start over (tap twice to confirm) puts all ten million pieces back.
