Clean The Map

Last night the city of Brightwater threw the biggest festival in its history. This morning there are 10,000,000 pieces of trash on its streets, and you're the only one who can clean it.

What you do

  • Pick it all up — every one of the ten million pieces is real: cups, cans, bottles, flyers, pizza boxes, balloons, glow sticks... The counter at the top shows how many are left.
  • Empty your bin, get paid — 54 recycling points around town pay for every load.
  • Better tools — from your bare hands to a litter picker, dustpan, shovel, rake, leaf blower, backpack vacuum, and finally a ride-on sweeper, a street sweeper truck and the Mega Vac.
  • Bigger bins and upgrades — bin depth, fast hands, long arms, running shoes, a trash magnet, a recycling drone and Rex the dog, who fetches trash for you.
  • Helpers — volunteers, street cleaners, robo-vacs and truck crews keep cleaning even while you're away.
  • Nine districts — the festival grounds, Main Street, Old Town, the park, the stadium, the river, the market, the harbour and the beach.
  • And more — jobs from the townspeople, golden trash, 27 lost treasures to find, trophies, combos and TURBO.

Free to play. Ads are optional: you can watch a short video for a bonus (double speed, double coins and more), and that's the only time you'll see one.
